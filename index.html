⭐ Published Paper

Potato Leaf Disease Classification using a Hybrid Deep Learning Model for Sustainable Agriculture

Published in the European Journal of Applied Science, Engineering and Technology (Vol. 4, Issue 6, pp. 1–15). Built a weighted ensemble of VGG19 and ResNet50V2 with ImageNet transfer learning to classify leaves as Early Blight, Late Blight or Healthy. Used augmentation and performance-based weighted averaging, which beat either model alone. Aimed at helping farmers detect crop disease early, even in remote areas.

📄 Published Paper

Artificial Intelligence for Ultrasound Image Acquisition and Interpretation in Regional Anesthesia: A Narrative Review of Current Evidence

Narrative review of AI-enabled devices in ultrasound-guided regional anesthesia (UGRA). Reported findings include about 93.5% accuracy in identifying eight anatomical landmarks across nine nerve block regions, better block-view acquisition and structure identification for non-experts using AI, and a lower reported rate of paresthesia on puncture. Concludes that larger multicenter randomized trials are needed. Co-authors: Amna Arif, Habib Ur Rehman, Irfan Khan and Naji Ullah. Applied Plant and Life Sciences (2026), 3: 49–63.

📄 Published Paper

Agentic AI in Real-World Systems: A Critical Review of Autonomous Workflows, Tool Integration, Applications, and Performance Evaluation

Critical review of 80 sources on agentic AI: how agents plan workflows, integrate tools, where they are applied, and how they are evaluated. Finds that none of 27 application cases reports a production deployment with measured outcomes, and that structural privilege separation was the only defence with near-zero attack success. Journal for Social Science Studies, 4(7), 142–241 (2026). First author. Co-authors: Hamid Ghous, Muhammad Zubair, Muzammil Hussain, Muhammad Akmal Shahzad and Ghulam Muhy Ud Deen Raee (corresponding author).
